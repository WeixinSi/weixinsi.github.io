description: >-
  On 20 August 2026, the China Computer Federation Technical Committee on
  Virtual Reality and Visualization Technology (CCF TCVRV), the Virtual Reality
  Technical Committee of the China Society of Image and Graphics (CSIG), and the
  Medical Simulation Technical Committee of the China Simulation Federation
  (CSF) jointly visited Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology for the
  “Intelligent Computing + X” Frontier Academic Exchange. Invited speakers
  included Prof. Yikun Zhang, Prof. Shanshan Wang, Prof. Ruizhen Hu, Prof.
  Hongming Shan, and Prof. Chu Han. Prof. Boyu Gao, Deputy Secretary-General of
  CCF TCVRV, and Prof. Yang Gao, Secretary of the CSF Medical Simulation
  Technical Committee, introduced their respective technical committees. Prof.
  Lequan Yu also joined the event as an invited speaker, and Prof. Weixin Si
  served as the Executive Chair.
