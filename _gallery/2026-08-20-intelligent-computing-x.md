description: >-
  On 20 August 2026, Prof. Weixin Si invited the China Computer Federation
  Technical Committee on Virtual Reality and Visualization Technology (CCF
  TCVRV), the Virtual Reality Technical Committee of the China Society of Image
  and Graphics (CSIG), and the Medical Simulation Technical Committee of the
  China Simulation Federation (CSF) to jointly visit the Shenzhen University of
  Advanced Technology for the “Intelligent Computing + X” Workshop. Invited
  speakers included Prof. Yikun Zhang, Prof. Shanshan Wang, Prof. Ruizhen Hu,
  Prof. Hongming Shan, and Prof. Chu Han. Prof. Yang Gao, Secretary of the CSF
  Medical Simulation Technical Committee, and Prof. Boyu Gao, Deputy
  Secretary-General of CCF TCVRV, Prof. Lequan Yu from Hong Kong University were
  also joined and organized this event.
